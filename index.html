How it works

Sending Bahamian dollars to someone in the Bahamas, step by step.

1
💵

You send USD or BSD

Pick your send-from currency and amount. We show the fee and the exact BSD your recipient gets before you confirm. Fee: $0.99 + 0.37% in the currency you send.

2
🏝️

Converted if needed, then moved locally

Sending USD? It converts at the mid-market rate (BSD is pegged 1:1) and then moves domestically in BSD. Sending BSD? It stays BSD the whole way — no FX. Either way it routes straight to a Bahamas bank or islandwise wallet.

3
⚡

They get BSD in seconds

Your recipient is credited in Bahamian dollars almost immediately (bank account or email/phone). You get a reference number. Arrives in seconds, not days.
